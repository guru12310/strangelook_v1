SAFETY IS A FEATURE, NOT A FOOTNOTE

Go with curiosity.
Keep your boundaries.

StrangeLook is designed to help people connect without giving up control. Identity checks reduce uncertainty, but no online verification can guarantee someone's behaviour.

Our safety approach ↗
✓
Identity verification

Age and identity check before joining the community. Sensitive ID documents should be handled by a trusted verification provider, not displayed publicly.

✓
Host approval

Requests are private. The plan creator chooses who to accept and can decline or cancel at any time.

✓
Report, block & support

Block unwanted contact, report unsafe behaviour and preserve relevant incident records securely.

✓
Public-meetup guidance

Meet in a busy public venue, arrange your own transport and share your plan with someone you trust.
